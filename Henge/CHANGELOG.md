## Henge 1.0

First release.

- **Presets.** Save your graphics settings as named presets and swap between them from the window or a macro. FPS Settings and Inari Graphics are built in, and your settings from before Henge are kept as "Original Settings".
- **More than the menu.** A preset keeps the Blizzard graphics sliders and the engine settings that tweak addons change, so those tweaks survive a swap.
- **Locations.** Pick a preset for Open World, Dungeon and Raid. Henge switches when you arrive and tells you in chat. Arenas use the Dungeon preset and battlegrounds the Raid preset.
- **Characters.** Any character can have its own locations; everyone else follows the account ones.
- **No surprises.** Henge only changes settings when you swap, log in, or change location. Change something in the game menu and the preset shows as modified, with a button to save it.
- **inari.** Presets you saved in inari are imported on first login.
